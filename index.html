A personal content workflow · English only

From an idea to content you have reviewed.

Content Factory 02 helps Mohamed Ghali prepare posts, images and videos. A person reviews the finished media, wording and destination before release.

Production runs on the operator’s own computer, with selected online services where authorized. The workflow brings drafting, media preparation and publishing checks together.

The working sequence

  1. 01

    Start with an idea

    Use an original idea or source material you have the right to use.

  2. 02

    Prepare the content

    Develop the words and media. Check factual claims where needed.

  3. 03

    Review the actual result

    Inspect the media, copy, destination and any required AI disclosure.

  4. 04

    Approve the next action

    Authorize private staging or a separate public release. Use prepared manual files when an automatic connection is unavailable.

Connected account use

YouTube, with a human decision.

The app uses YouTube API Services to identify the connected channel, inspect the status of its own uploads and upload an exactly reviewed video privately.

Public release or scheduling needs a separate explicit human approval and suitable platform permissions. Some publication functions are still being verified. A prepared file or private upload is not confirmation of public publication.
